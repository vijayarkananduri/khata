A little clarity, without the noise

The useful parts
of a money app.

Record spending and income, spot patterns, and keep everyday essentials in view. Khata stays a ledger—not a bank, a social network, or a financial adviser.

01

See where it went

Daily, weekly, monthly and all-time totals sit beside a readable category breakdown and a year-at-a-glance spending heatmap.

02

Get a gentle restock nudge

Log a regular purchase a few times and Khata estimates its rhythm on-device. If a litre of milk usually lasts two days, it can remind you to check before you run out. Nudges are optional.

03

Quotes, in the language you choose

Movie and meme quote lines are available in five languages, in the original script or transliteration.

HindiTeluguTamilMalayalamKannada

These are quote languages; the app interface itself is not translated into all five.

04

Your records stay yours

No bank linking, account or cloud sync. Export the full transaction history as CSV when you want a copy or need to move it.

Built for ordinary routines

A nudge before
the last litre.

Khata notices repeated entries for things like milk, groceries or fuel. It estimates how long they tend to last and gives you a local reminder near the next likely purchase—not an order, just a heads-up.
