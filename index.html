What does a restock nudge do?

After a few repeat entries, Khata estimates how long an item tends to last. If a litre of milk usually lasts two days, it can remind you to check the fridge around day two. It’s optional and calculated on your phone.

See what each widget shows

Daily shows today’s spend. Weekly covers the last seven days. Monthly and all-time show their respective totals. The budget ring tracks monthly progress, and the quote widget shows a short line on your home screen. Widgets refresh when you change the app theme.
